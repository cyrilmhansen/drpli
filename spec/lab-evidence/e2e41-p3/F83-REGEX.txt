### cpmwork.check_f83 rejected extensionless CP/M names -- reproduction (E2E-41/P3b, [C281])
python: 3.14.8  on linux
old pattern: ^([A-Za-z0-9_]{1,8})(?:\.([A-Za-z0-9_]{1,3})?)$
  the '?' sits INSIDE the group: the DOT is mandatory, the extension optional
new pattern: ^([A-Za-z0-9_]{1,8})(?:\.([A-Za-z0-9_]{1,3}))?$
  the whole '.EXT' is optional, which is what CP/M's F8.3 grammar says

### the two names that matter
  old: check_f83('E16OUT') -> SafetyError illegal CP/M F8.3 name 'E16OUT'
  old: check_f83('OUT') -> SafetyError illegal CP/M F8.3 name 'OUT'
  new: check_f83('E16OUT') -> 'E16OUT' (group(2) is None, which is what the function's own normalisation branch reads)
  new: check_f83('e16out.dat') -> 'E16OUT.DAT'  (still upper-cases both fields)
