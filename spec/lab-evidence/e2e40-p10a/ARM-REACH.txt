CONSEQUENCE FOR [C272]'s SCOPE.  MB34 and MB35 are real witnesses: a PARENTHESIZED right operand
whose inner operator is MUL arrives at this arm and is evaluated LEFT-first by DR (MB34's own
relocation order is 0x1, 0x1, 0x3 = n, n, m -- the left operand's LHLD is the first expression
fixup) and stays byte-identical after P10.  R722 and LR19 are NOT witnesses about this arm: neither
reaches it, so neither can confirm or deny anything about `paren_add_right'.  P10's note and code
comment listed all four as the bound; the correct bound is MB34/MB35 for MUL, and UNMEASURED at this
arm for DIV, SUB, EXP and for a parenthesized literal.  P10a corrects the attribution; the programs
themselves are untouched and remain byte-exact specimens of whatever route they do take.

ADDENDUM [P10a]: POSITIVE IDENTIFICATION, NOT ABSENCE
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Absence of a trace line is weak evidence -- it can mean "different route" or "instrument in
the wrong place" (P9a's lesson, [C271]).  Both non-witnesses are therefore identified
positively as well:

  LR19 `y = x + (-32768)'
      codegen.ml has FOUR `Ast.Ebinary' arms before the generic FIXED ADD arm, at lines
      3886 (mixed DEC), 3903 (out-of-range integer literal, guarded by `e25_bin_routes
      (e25_is_dec st) op l r'), 3919 (call on the right, [C141]) and then the generic arm
      itself.  `over_lit_operand' (line 1734) unwraps `Econd' and recognizes
      `Eprefix (Minus, Enumber)', so LR19's right operand is claimed by the arm at 3903.
      The emitted bytes agree with that arm's `None, Some' branch and not with the generic
      arm's: `LHLD x / PUSH H / LXI H,<cell> / MVI A,05 / CALL / CALL / POP D / DAD D /
      SHLD y' -- the left operand is loaded first and PARKED, and the literal is converted
      into a signed decimal cell (the [E2E-25/C178] route, LR11/LR17/LR18/LR19 are its own
      four specimens).  So LR19 never reaches the arm that carries `paren_add_right'.
      Note what its bytes do NOT show: they show no XCHG at all, so P9's table row
      "LR19 (literal right, in parens) | XCHG | PUSH H" describes neither the route nor the
      order.  It is not a left-first witness either: it is a different arm.

  R722 `a = b + (c/d)' with four `fixed dec(9,2)' operands
      Its DECIMAL precision makes it a mixed-DEC program, claimed before the generic arm.
      Positive evidence in the images themselves: the EXTERNAL name records of DR's golden
      `golden/e18/MB30/R722.REL' are `?DDVOP ?PNVOP ?START ?SYSPR', and this compiler's
      rebuilt image lists exactly the same four.  `?DDVOP' is DR's decimal operate routine;
      a FIXED-binary XCHG/PUSH ladder for `b + (c/d)' would carry none of these names.
      MB34's EXTERNALs by contrast are `?IM22N ?PNCOP ?START' -- integer conversion, no
      decimal operator -- and MB34 DOES reach the generic arm, with the trace confirming it.

So: R722 and LR19 are excluded by two independent kinds of evidence (trace absence from a
site proven live by three other programs in the same build, plus route identification from
code reading and from the images' own EXTERNAL records / instruction shapes).
