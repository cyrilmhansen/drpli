BIAS VALUES WITH A DR ARTIFACT AT A MEMBER: **26 distinct** -- 1 established in P8 (+5, E42P1), 24
more in P8a, 1 more in P8b (-32767, E44A1).  ACCOUNTING RULE, so the number is reproducible and not a
hand list: take the rows of this file whose dr_instruction_pattern begins LHLD (the ladder) and whose
statement is a self-update on an internal member, decode k from DR's own operand in that column
(LXI B,<hex> -> signed 16-bit; "INX H x n" -> +n; "DCX H x n" -> -n), and count distinct k.  That
gives 1 / 24 / 1 per episode and 26 overall; the 28 P8a ladder rows cover 25 distinct biases because
three of them reuse a bias already sampled (+5 in E43L6 and E43L7, +16 in E43L4 and E43B16, +17 in E43L5 and E43B17), so the
ladder rows total **30** programs: 28 from P8a, plus P8's E42P1, plus P8b's E44A1.  E42P2
and E43M1 (literal stores) and E44B1/C1/D1 (conversion route, two of them on an ordinary variable) are
NOT ladder rows and contribute no bias.  Panel: 14 of the 30 ladder rows are registered byte-exact
specimens, and between them they pin **12 of the 26 biases** (-32767, -9, -8, -1, +3, +4, +5, +16, +17,
+100, +30000, +32767); the other 14 biases are evidence in golden/e43b, cited in COVERAGE.tsv, but not
individually on the panel.
NOT SAMPLED ANYWHERE AT A MEMBER: +1, +2, -2, -5, -30 (C268's first draft listed these; they have no
artifact and are struck -- note -1 IS sampled, as E43L3's DCX H x1, so the "+/-1" phrasing used in
earlier drafts was wrong at the negative end), and anything above +32767 or below -32768.
