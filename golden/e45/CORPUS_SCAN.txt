E2E-40/P9 -- BLAST RADIUS, MEASURED OVER THE WHOLE GOLDEN CORPUS
================================================================
Tool: golden/e45/scan_tails.py (run: `python3 golden/e45/scan_tails.py' from the repo root, ~3 min).
It reads all 2050 .REL files through tools/dis80.code_and_xrefs and counts DR's two
operand-preservation tails by opcode sequence:
  park  E5 2A . . D1 19   PUSH H / LHLD <right> / POP D / DAD D
  swap  EB 2A . . 19      XCHG / LHLD <right> / DAD D
and flags a site whose preceding byte is 19 (DAD D), i.e. a SECOND add inside one expression --
the only population a rule keyed on a compound left operand could possibly change.

  park sites, all                                    44   (in 154 files)
  swap sites, all                                   128   (in 154 files)
  park sites at a second add                         11 listings
  swap sites at a second add                          5 listings

LISTINGS ARE NOT PROGRAMS -- and the duplication is not uniform, which is the trap.  Older runtime
programs have a second copy under golden/e32rt_<NAME>/ (E44A1 appears twice, as do E40S2 and E40S4),
but E45S1 appears exactly ONCE, so dividing the listing count by two is wrong.  Every listing is also
DR's golden, not my build's output, whatever the panel stores alongside it.  Deduplicated by base
name, the 11 + 5 listings are 9 programs:
  second add, PARK:  E40S2, E40S4, E45S1, E45S4, E45S6, E45S7          (6 programs)
  second add, SWAP:  D12, E45S2, E45S3                                  (3 programs)
and the 154/154 file counts are also inflated by those copies, so the interesting number is not
there either.

WHY THIS IS THE COLLATERAL PROOF.  The nine programs above are, between them, the entire set of DR
samples where the choice at a second add has ever been observed.  Six of them are this slice's own
probes, two are the divergences it closed, and the ninth -- golden/e2e182/D12, source `return (t +
(g(3) + g(3))) + g(3)' read out of DR's listing, ordinary scalars throughout -- is DR's XCHG behind a
compound left, which the new rule must and does leave alone.  Every one of those nine is byte-exact
against DR on the current build, and 982/982 panel specimens pass with 0 failures, with the corpus
census recomputing to 0 DIFFERS / 0 UNEXPECTED.  Nothing outside this set was reachable by the change.

The remaining 44-11 = 33 park listings are FIRST adds, where this compiler already pushed for the
reason [C260] recorded (both operands members) -- a reason that coincides with the true rule on every
sample that has ever shown it.

WHAT THIS SCAN DOES AND DOES NOT MEASURE, stated plainly because an earlier paragraph of mine
implied otherwise.  It reads the COMMITTED DR GOLDENS ONLY.  Re-running it after a codegen change
measures nothing about my build -- DR's files did not move -- so the numbers above are a description
of the corpus and of the rule's reach, not evidence that the fix worked.  Post-change parity is
established a different way and is stated with the instrument that produced it: this compiler's own
output for E45S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S7, S8, E40S2 and E40S4 is 256/256 whole-file identical to DR
(0 differing bytes each, verified with cmp and with reldump CODE/DATA sizes), and the panel reports
982 byte-exact / 0 fail with 9/9 pinned divergences and 45/45 runtime rows.
